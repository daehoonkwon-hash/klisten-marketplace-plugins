# Capability boundaries

Prepare supplied-data transaction structure, right-to-operate checks, sanctions flags, tariff estimates and a dossier.

- UNVERIFIED source, missing/expired official feeds or supplied regulatory claims never authorize a transaction.
- No official sanctions clearance, current government-license validation, legal advice, contract signature, regulatory filing, FX dealing or transfer.
- Caller-supplied org/owner or sourceEvidence overrides are forbidden; subjectKey is only a local lookup label, never a real key/password/token.

These source imports and local tests do not establish a released production product, OAuth client, carrier/government authority or marketplace approval.
